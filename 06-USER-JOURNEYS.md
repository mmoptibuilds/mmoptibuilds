# User journeys

## Custom PC or workstation

1. Arrive on a use-case page.
2. Understand the requirement-led, no-stock model.
3. Choose workload, existing preferences, private budget range, location, assembly need, and timing.
4. Submit after server-side Turnstile validation.
5. Receive an on-screen reference ID and response expectation without a guaranteed deadline.
6. Owner researches availability and prepares a tailored quote.

## Enterprise sourcing

1. Arrive through a part-number or enterprise-intent query.
2. See the sourcing-and-delivery boundary immediately.
3. Provide manufacturer, exact part/SKU, quantity, condition, location, required date, warranty, tax/PO needs, and documents.
4. Owner validates the specification and suppliers manually before quoting.

## Focused website

1. Arrive on business website or redesign page.
2. See the practical outcome: credibility, clarity, mobile usability, and conversion.
3. Explain organization, audience, primary action, required pages, current assets, domain/hosting state, and timing.
4. Owner reviews scope and replies manually with questions or a quote.

## Premium website

1. Enter through Studio positioning, work, or referral.
2. Explore art direction and case-study reasoning without blocked navigation or forced motion.
3. Select bespoke mode and describe ambitions, differentiation, content, assets, and approval process.
4. Owner qualifies fit before proposal and discovery.

## Owner operations

1. Receive a verified owner notification.
2. Open the authenticated dashboard.
3. Review full submission, consent version, source route, spam signals, and history.
4. Set status, priority, notes, quote amount privately, and next follow-up date.
5. Reply manually from email; record the action.
6. Close as won, lost, spam, duplicate, or archived.

## Failure journeys

Every form preserves non-sensitive input after a recoverable error, identifies the field that needs attention, supports retry, and never creates duplicate inquiries from repeated taps.
