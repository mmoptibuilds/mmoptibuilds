# Security policy

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Report it privately to the security contact designated by the owner before launch. Include affected route, impact, reproduction steps, and supporting evidence without accessing or retaining other people's data.

Production secrets, personal inquiry data, supplier terms, and internal infrastructure details must not be pasted into tickets, chat prompts, screenshots, analytics, or logs. Rotate any credential that may have been exposed.

Supported security reviews cover the current production release. The project should triage critical issues immediately, high issues within two business days, and lower issues according to risk. These targets are internal goals, not a public guarantee.
